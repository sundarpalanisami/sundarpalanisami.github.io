About Me

Highly analytical and performance-driven Facade BIM Coordinator and Mechanical Engineer with 10+ years of comprehensive experience delivering high-profile data centers, high-rise residential towers, and luxury villa developments across the UAE and India.

Documented success managing end-to-end BIM workflows in strict compliance with ISO 19650 standards, steering cross-functional coordination, and managing lifecycle facade models from initial shop drawings to final as-built documentation.

Proven expert in executing advanced clash detection, multi-disciplinary resolution, and precise material quantity takeoff extraction to optimize project timelines and reduce structural field errors.

Skilled in leveraging cutting-edge BIM tools including Revit, Navisworks, and Dynamo to drive design optimization, enhance collaboration, and ensure seamless integration of facade systems with architectural and structural elements. Adept at leading project teams through complex BIM processes, fostering stakeholder communication, and delivering high-quality deliverables that meet stringent project requirements.
